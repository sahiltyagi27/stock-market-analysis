Reaction = close(report date − 7cal days) → close(report date) → close(report date + 7cal days). Rev/NII YoY is Net Interest/Premium Income growth for banks & insurers, not topline revenue.
Sourced from company press releases and financial news (NSE/BSE direct access is policy-blocked in the environment this data was researched in); prices from Kite daily candles. Pearson r (shown above) is computed live in your browser from whatever rows are currently loaded, so it updates automatically as more quarters get seeded. Add data with go run ./cmd/earnings-reaction --seed in the repo, then click Refresh — no restart needed.
